The inaugural **Rocky Mountain Urban and Real Estate (RMURE) Conference** was a huge success.

Held June 5–7, 2026 at the [Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity](https://www.banffcentre.ca/) in Banff, Alberta, RMURE brought together scholars working in urban and real estate economics for three days of research and discussion. The program featured fifteen papers across six sessions — housing supply and affordability, real estate markets and finance, sorting and agglomeration in cities, housing and urban structure, spatial equilibrium and the environment, and commercial real estate — along with a keynote address by [Nathaniel Baum-Snow](https://sites.google.com/site/baumsnow/home) (University of Toronto) on housing affordability and the mobility of renters.

The conference is organized by [Jay Hyun](https://sites.google.com/site/jungsikhyunecon), James Macek, and [Nathan Schiff](https://nathanschiff.com/), and hosted by the Department of Marketing, Business Economics and Law at the [Alberta School of Business](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/index.html), University of Alberta. We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Alberta School of Business, the [U of A Cities Institute](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/cities-institute/index.html), and the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada.
